This paper conducted a comprehensive survey spanning from established text embedding methods to advanced embedding techniques in multilingual, long-context, and cross-modal domains. It systematizes the paradigm shift from specialized encoders like BERT to general-purpose LLMs for generating high-quality text embeddings.

The paper systematically summarizes and categorizes two core methodologies: direct prompting for tuning-free embeddings and data-centric fine-tuning to optimize model with diverse objectives and datasets.
