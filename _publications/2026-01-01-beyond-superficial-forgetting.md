This paper proposes KUnBR, a novel machine unlearning framework designed to thoroughly remove harmful knowledge from LLMs. It leverages Knowledge Density Estimation to pinpoint the layers richest in harmful knowledge, and a Block Re-insertion strategy for thorough unlearning.

The method achieves state-of-the-art performance on unlearning benchmarks, robustly resisting knowledge recovery while preserving model general utility.
