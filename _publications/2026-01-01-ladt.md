This paper proposes LADT, a novel paradigm that localizes GUI elements via iterative ancestor-to-descendant traversal of the DOM tree to enable coarse-to-fine grounding. It designs a Subsumption-Aware Hierarchical Reward that combines sparse inbox rewards with dense structural penalties to enforce coherent localization paths and correct containment relationships.

The method achieves SOTA performance on three Web/GUI benchmarks with strong cross-platform generalization.
