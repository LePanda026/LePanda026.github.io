This paper proposes DPEPO, a novel agent exploration paradigm that enables simultaneous exploration of multiple environments for information acquisition and action execution, coupled with a diversity-driven reward mechanism to encourage efficient and diverse exploration during RL training.

The method achieves state-of-the-art (SOTA) results on ALF-World and ScienceWorld, with demonstrated strong robustness and generalization on out-of-domain tasks.
